Lilith Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Lilith Puzzle is provided by Tien Dao. We respect your family’s privacy. The app works without an account and stores your puzzle photos, voice recordings, imported music, and preferences on your device.

Information used by the app

Lilith Puzzle uses only the content you choose to provide:

  • Photos: You can select up to 50 photos using Apple’s system photo picker. The app saves resized copies to create puzzles. It does not request access to your entire photo library.
  • Voice recordings: You can optionally record messages for encouragement and puzzle feedback. Microphone access is requested when you choose to record.
  • Music: You can optionally select an audio file using Apple’s system file picker. The app saves a copy to play during puzzles.
  • Preferences: The app saves settings such as puzzle difficulty, piece shape, rotation, and sound preferences.

This information supports the app’s features. We do not receive or have remote access to this content.

Sharing, advertising, and tracking

Lilith Puzzle does not upload your photos, recordings, or music to servers operated by us. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers.

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not request your name, email address, location, or an account.

Children’s privacy

Lilith Puzzle is designed for children and families. We do not collect personal information from children through the app.

Parents and guardians can manage puzzle photos, voice recordings, imported music, and settings in the Parent section. We encourage adults to supervise the selection of photos and the creation of recordings.

Storage and backups

Imported content remains in the app’s storage until you remove or replace it, or delete the app.

Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Apple, such as iCloud Backup or computer backups. These backups are controlled by your device settings and Apple’s services. Lilith Puzzle does not provide its own cloud storage or synchronisation service.

Your choices and deleting information

You can:

  • Remove puzzle photos in the Parent section.
  • Remove voice recordings and imported music under Parent section → Sound, music & recordings.
  • Revoke microphone permission in your device’s Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove its locally stored content and preferences. Offloading the app may retain its data.

Removing a photo or music file from Lilith Puzzle does not delete the original from your photo library or Files. Copies in existing device backups may remain until those backups are deleted or updated.

Apple services

The app uses Apple’s system photo picker, file picker, microphone permissions, and audio features. Apple may process information through its operating system, App Store, diagnostics, or backup services according to your settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy.
