Lilith Puzzle V1.0

Turns familiar pictures into playful puzzles with Lilith Puzzle.
Add your favourite photos, choose a challenge, and tap a picture to play. A simple picture-based home screen makes choosing a puzzle easy.
• Create puzzles from up to 10 photos
• Choose from 4 to 36 pieces
• Explore rectangular, classic interlocking, and irregular shapes
• Add an optional rotation challenge with 90° or 45° turns
• Show a picture hint whenever a little help is needed
• Record a familiar voice for encouragement, celebrations, and gentle try-again feedback
• Import your own background music
• Manage photos, difficulty, and sound in a separate parent section
Photos, recordings, and imported music are stored on your device. No account is required.

Privacy:
  Lilith Puzzle does not collect, store, or upload any personal data. All drawings and photos remain on the device.
